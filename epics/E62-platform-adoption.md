## Description

Make the platform the easiest path for teams, and measure whether they take it.

## Outcome

Golden-path templates and docs exist, a pilot team has shipped a model on the platform, and adoption is tracked against the success metrics.

## Scope (in)

- Golden-path project templates and an example project.
- Documentation and tutorials.
- Onboarding of a first pilot team, with feedback fed back into the platform.
- Migration plan for existing projects.
- Support model and platform service levels.
- Intake and prioritisation of platform requests.
- Adoption metrics reviewed against the success metrics.

## Scope (out)

- Mandating adoption; that is an organisational decision.

## Decisions

- **Choose the pilot team and the support model.**
